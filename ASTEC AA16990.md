This image shows the internal view of an ASTEC AA16990 power supply unit (PSU) from a Macintosh Quadra 610 computer, labeled with key components and output voltage markings.[1][2]

**Key Features**
The PSU is rated at 86W total power, supporting universal input from 100-240V AC. It features a flyback transformer design with multiple electrolytic capacitors, inductors, and rectifier diodes typical for 1990s switch-mode PSUs.[3][4][5][1]

**Voltage Outputs**
Labeled rails include +5V (two lines), +12V, -12V, +34.6V (likely for CRT or backplane), matching Quadra 610 requirements for logic board, drives, and optional video.[6][1]
